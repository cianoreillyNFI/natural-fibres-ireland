Hemp licence guide

A short orientation to the current cultivation route in Ireland.

The short answer

You need a controlled-drug licence to cultivate hemp in Ireland. The HPRA manages the application process on behalf of the Department of Health.

Before you apply

  • Check the variety is in the EU Common Catalogue.
  • Use certified seed and keep the seed label and purchase documents.
  • Have your cultivation-site details ready.
  • Use the current HPRA application form and guidance.

Current THC threshold

DAFM stated to the Oireachtas on 8 July 2026 that permitted cultivation is limited to varieties with less than 0.3% THC that are registered in the EU Common Catalogue.
