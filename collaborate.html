Bring a real piece of the value chain.

We are interested in practical collaborators across agriculture, processing, research, manufacturing, design, construction, procurement and public policy.

Farmers & land managers

Supply, harvesting constraints, realistic returns and trial opportunities.

Processors & machinery suppliers

Decortication, fibre opening, drying, milling, non-wovens and related capability.

Manufacturers

Defined material requirements, substitution opportunities and production trials.

Researchers & test centres

Materials science, agronomy, testing, LCA, fire, hygrothermal and manufacturing expertise.

Designers & specifiers

Performance requirements that connect material development to real projects.

Public bodies & funders

Regional development, skills, demonstrators, infrastructure and evidence-led policy.

A useful first conversation

Specific beats broad.

The strongest enquiries normally contain at least one real asset or constraint: a feedstock, a machine, a manufacturer, a test capability, a buyer, a site or a defined material problem.
