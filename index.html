Our mission

Enable Ireland to make more from its own fibres.

We want natural fibres to become a practical part of Ireland's modern industrial base: sourced responsibly, processed to consistent standards and converted into products that solve real problems.

The case for action

Ireland has the raw material. The missing piece is the value chain.

A fibre economy is not created by one crop, one machine or one research project. It depends on coordinated supply, processing, quality, logistics, testing, product development and demand.

Natural Fibres Ireland focuses on that enabling layer: helping good ideas cross the gap between raw biological resources and repeatable industrial materials.

How we work

Principles for building a credible fibre economy.

Commercial reality

Start with a customer problem, technical requirements and willingness to pay.

Whole-chain thinking

Design cultivation, harvesting, processing and product development together.

Regional value creation

Keep more processing, knowledge and manufacturing activity close to where resources arise.

Evidence before claims

Natural does not automatically mean sustainable. Measure performance, durability, cost, emissions and end of life.

Open collaboration

Use the expertise that already exists and build new infrastructure only where the gap is real.

What needs to work

A fibre sector only works when the whole chain works.

Known feedstocksVolumes, quality, locations, seasonality and realistic farm economics.
Accessible processingMachinery and know-how that can convert raw biomass into repeatable inputs.
Tested materialsIndependent evidence for the performance claims that matter in each market.
Manufacturing routesCompanies able to trial and adopt fibres within scalable processes.
Repeat customersDemand strong enough to survive beyond a grant-funded demonstrator.
