About 11 hectares of hemp. A value chain still to build.

In July 2026, the Department of Agriculture told the Oireachtas that approximately 11 hectares of hemp were recorded through BISS. The same evidence pointed to stable markets, processing capacity and supply-chain collaboration as the conditions for scale.

Source: DAFM opening statement, 8 July 2026 ↗

The issue is not whether Ireland can grow fibre

It is whether fibre can move reliably from a farm or feedstock stream into a specification, a processor and a repeat customer.

Natural Fibres Ireland is focused on that middle ground: the practical links between feedstock, processing, testing, manufacturing and market.

Carbon is part of the story, not a shortcut

Plants absorb CO₂ as they grow. That does not automatically create a carbon credit.

EU rules look at additionality, measurement, net climate benefit, storage and independent verification. We have put together a short explainer on what may matter for farmers, long-lived fibre products and biochar.
