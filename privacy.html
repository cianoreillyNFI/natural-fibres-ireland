Privacy

A simple website should have a simple data footprint.

This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. The only personal information we receive is information you choose to send by email or through a form.

Contact and form data

If you contact Natural Fibres Ireland, we use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss projects, trials or sector activity with you.

Website forms are delivered using FormSubmit.co, a third-party form delivery service. FormSubmit's published help information states that submissions may be retained for up to 30 days for delivery/recovery purposes. Do not use the forms for sensitive personal information.

Sharing

Natural Fibres Ireland will not pass the substance of your enquiry or contact details to another organisation without your permission, unless required by law.

Cookies and analytics

Natural Fibres Ireland does not currently run its own analytics or advertising tracking on this static website. Third-party services you choose to open, such as official source websites or the form provider, operate under their own policies.
