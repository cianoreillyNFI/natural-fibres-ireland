Thinking about fibre on your farm?

Start with the buyer, the processor and the harvest plan — not with the seed catalogue.

The honest position

Irish natural-fibre supply chains are still developing. We would rather say “not yet” than encourage a crop without a realistic outlet.

Crop & fibre options

What each one asks of you

Hemp suits Irish growing conditions, but commercial value depends on processing access. Read the licence guide.

Flax has a clear fibre history in Ireland; the modern challenge is a viable retting and fibre-preparation chain.

Wool is already on Irish farms. The question is how more grades can reach higher-value material uses.

Willow is a perennial woody crop with material, cellulose and biochar possibilities alongside established biomass uses.

Nettle is earlier-stage and should be treated as a fibre-development opportunity, not an established farm market.

8 JULY 2026

Hemp in the current Irish system

DAFM told the Oireachtas that hemp is eligible under BISS, that eligible varieties must be in the EU Common Catalogue and have less than 0.3% THC, and that approximately 11 hectares were recorded through BISS in 2026.

The same evidence stressed stable markets and processing capacity as conditions for scale.
