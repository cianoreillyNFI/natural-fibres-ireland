Carbon and natural fibres

Plants take carbon dioxide from the atmosphere as they grow. That does not mean every crop creates a carbon credit.

The emerging EU system asks harder questions: is the climate benefit additional, how is it measured, what emissions are associated with it, how long is carbon stored, and who verifies the result?

A tonne of CO₂ absorbed by a crop is not automatically a tradable unit.

Certification depends on an approved methodology and independent verification. Natural Fibres Ireland does not issue, certify, broker or sell carbon credits.

LAND

Carbon farming

The European Commission adopted methodologies in July 2026 covering agriculture and agroforestry on mineral soils, peatland restoration and afforestation.

A fibre crop such as hemp or flax might form part of a qualifying land-management system, but growing the crop on its own does not automatically generate a certified unit.

PRODUCTS

Carbon stored in materials

The EU CRCF Regulation covers carbon stored for at least 35 years in long-lasting products. The Commission specifically points to bio-based construction products made from timber and agricultural crops.

A dedicated methodology for carbon storage in buildings is planned for the end of 2026. Until that exists, we would not tell anyone that a hemp, flax, wool or willow product automatically earns credits.

RESIDUES

Biochar

Biochar carbon removal is further ahead. The EU adopted a dedicated methodology in 2026.

That may be relevant where qualifying fibre or woody residues enter a compliant pyrolysis and storage pathway. Feedstock, process emissions, permanence and monitoring still have to meet the methodology.

IRELAND · MAY 2026

The Irish framework is still developing

DAFM published the report from its consultation on a National Carbon Farming Framework in May 2026.

That means this is a live policy area. There is no single guaranteed carbon price and no basis for telling an Irish farmer that planting a particular fibre crop will automatically create a payment.

Three plain-English answers

Does hemp automatically create carbon credits?

No. Carbon absorbed during growth is only one part of the calculation.

Could natural-fibre products matter?

Potentially. Long-lived bio-based construction products are explicitly within the EU framework, but the buildings methodology is still being developed.

Is this the EU ETS?

No. EU ETS allowances are part of a mandatory cap-and-trade market for covered emitters. This page is about carbon removals, carbon farming and storage certification.
