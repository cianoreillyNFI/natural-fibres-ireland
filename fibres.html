The material base

Different fibres. Different strengths.

The goal is not to make one feedstock answer every problem. It is to understand where each Irish fibre can deliver useful performance and a credible route to scale.

Material discipline

Natural is a starting point, not a performance claim.

The design question is not “is it 100% natural?” but “does the system deliver better whole-life performance with a credible route to scale?”

That means testing the things that matter for the product: moisture, fire, strength, durability, toxicity, recyclability, emissions, cost and manufacturing compatibility.
