Why this exists

The missing middle

Ireland has farms, biomass, research capability and companies already using natural materials. What is still thin is the route between them.

Natural Fibres Ireland is focused on that route: finding feedstock, understanding processing, proving material quality and connecting it to a real user.

The chain

Not a slogan. A sequence that has to work.
