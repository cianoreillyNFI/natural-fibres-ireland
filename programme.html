The programme

Build the infrastructure around the material.

Our programme focuses on the practical work required to move from scattered fibre activity to repeatable value chains.

01

Map

Feedstock & capability intelligence

Build a living picture of growers, biomass streams, machinery, processors, test facilities, manufacturers, buyers and regional clusters.

02

Process

Primary processing infrastructure

Identify practical routes for harvesting, conditioning, drying, retting, decortication, opening, cleaning, grading, milling and fractionation.

03

Engineer

Materials & product development

Work backwards from real performance requirements to create useful fibres, blends, formats, binders and intermediate materials.

04

Prove

Demonstration & validation

Use prototypes, independent testing, life-cycle assessment and field demonstrations to separate credible products from attractive ideas.

05

Equip

Skills & enterprise readiness

Build practical capability among growers, technicians, designers, processors and manufacturers.

06

Scale

Markets, policy & procurement

Address customer demand, standards, finance, procurement and policy barriers that prevent adoption beyond pilot scale.

Near-term priorities

Irish fibre map

Identify real feedstocks, capability and gaps.

Processing pilots

Prove routes for a small number of priority fibres.

Flagship demonstrators

Build a few serious products with measurable performance and a named market need.

A platform, not a silo

Make existing organisations more effective.

Natural Fibres Ireland does not need to duplicate farmer bodies, universities, technology centres, public agencies or manufacturers. It should connect them around opportunities that no single organisation can deliver alone.

Where expertise already exists, use it. Where a genuine gap exists—especially in processing, coordination or demonstration—build the evidence needed to fill it.
